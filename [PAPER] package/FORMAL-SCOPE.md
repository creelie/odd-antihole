# Proof and verification scope

The paper proves strong chromatic-choosability for every odd antihole and deduces the extremal odd-antihole characterization in odd-hole-free critical graphs.

The manuscript supplies a general proof for every integer ell >= 3. Its path and cycle lemmas lead to the main list-colouring theorem. The critical-graph consequence is proved in the manuscript, with its source credited to Fiujlaali and McDonald.

The supplied Lean development proves the main antihole theorem and its supporting path, cycle and list-colouring lemmas. The critical-graph consequence in Section 6 is a written proof and is not formalised. The successful prior repository CI is recorded in verification/previous-ci.json; no new local Lean rebuild is claimed by this package.

The mathematical proof bodies have not been changed by this packaging revision. It does not claim a new conjecture beyond the statements in the manuscript.
